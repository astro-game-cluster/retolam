You only need to focus on a few key elements to master the rhythm battle. Pay close attention to these indicators to track your performance and progress.

4. The Rules of the World: Core Mechanics

The game operates on a simple, yet demanding, set of rules. Understanding these mechanics is essential for surviving the intense songs.

  • The Timing Window: The core mechanic is precise timing. You must press the correct arrow key exactly when the moving note overlaps the static arrow on the screen. Pressing too early, too late, or the wrong key will result in a Miss or a low-scoring hit, punishing you with energy loss.

  • The Energy Economy: Your energy bar is your life. If you hit notes successfully and maintain a good tempo, your energy increases or stays stable. If you miss notes or hit them poorly, the energy bar rapidly shifts toward your opponent, meaning you must recover quickly with accurate playing.

FNF Hellbeats is a rhythm game mod inspired by the popular FNF (Friday Night Funkin’) series. You play as Boyfriend, who, along with Girlfriend, has been darkened for this challenge. Your goal is to hit the corresponding arrow keys in time with the music to match the floating notes on the screen and out-rhythm your demonic opponents. Maintain your energy meter until the song ends to win!

The core controls are the four arrow keys on your keyboard (Up, Down, Left, Right). When a floating note aligns with the stationary arrows at the top of the screen, press the corresponding arrow key quickly and accurately. Timing is everything!

The mod features two main modes: Story Mode and Free Play Mode. Story Mode allows you to progress through the weeks on three difficulty settings (easy, medium, hard). Free Play Mode is designed for experts and allows you to select and practice any song you’ve unlocked.

The game offers three difficulty levels in Story Mode: Easy, Medium, and Hard. If you are new to rhythm games, we recommend starting with Easy to get familiar with the beats. Free Play Mode is generally much more challenging and recommended for experienced players.

If the game is running slowly or notes are not registering correctly, try the following steps: 1. Clear your browser cache and cookies. 2. Ensure your browser is updated to the latest version (Chrome or Firefox are usually best). 3. Close other demanding tabs or applications running in the background. 4. Disable any browser extensions that might interfere with performance.

As FNF Hellbeats is an iframe/browser-based game, performance issues are often related to latency. If the notes seem out of sync with the music, look for a ‘Calibration’ or ‘Sync’ option in the main menu or settings. If one is not explicitly available, try refreshing the page, as this often resets the game’s timing.

Since this is an iframe mod, your progress is typically saved locally in your browser’s data (Local Storage). If you clear your browser’s cache or cookies entirely, you might lose your progress, unlocked songs, and high scores. We recommend avoiding clearing your data unless absolutely necessary.

Unfortunately, FNF Hellbeats is generally a single-player rhythm experience. There is no official built-in multiplayer or co-op mode. Your focus is on mastering the difficult beats and improving your personal high scores.
